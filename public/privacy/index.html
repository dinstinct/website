Privacy, in plain words.

Dion Labs is a one-person independent software lab in Italy. This page covers the lab sites and small products unless a product gives you a more specific notice.

What the sites collect

The public sites use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless service that gives us aggregate traffic measurements. Cloudflare also processes ordinary request data needed to serve and protect the sites. We do not run advertising trackers.

When you give us information

Boxie's beta-tester form collects the email address and eligibility answers you submit so we can review and contact testers. Boxie's product privacy page explains its mailbox, optional cloud-vault, feedback, and deletion flows in more detail.

FolioDuet offers Google sign-in. Google and the service providers used by the app process account and service data needed to sign you in and run the product. The app shows its own notice where more detail is needed.

If you email us, we receive your address, message, and normal email metadata. We use them to answer you and handle the request.

Retention and your choices

We keep submitted information only as long as needed for its stated purpose, security, legal obligations, or the product-specific period shown when you submit it. You can ask for access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

Contact

Email support@dionlabs.ai with privacy questions. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or private mailbox contents.
